About This Resource

An Evidence Ticket method for organizing clues, built around a shared Sugargoo spreadsheet.

What this resource is

This is an independent informational and navigation resource for people who use a Sugargoo spreadsheet. It offers an Evidence Ticket method — source cue, visible option, current context, open question, and stop condition — so each clue is organized as an observation instead of turned into a guess.

What this resource is not

This resource is not the official Sugargoo website, and it is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated by Sugargoo or Google. It is not a store, a product database, a QC certification, a shopping agent, an order tracker, a fee calculator, or a purchase guarantee.

The Evidence Ticket method

Everything here follows five fields: Source cue (where the clue came from), Visible option (what the page clearly shows), Current context (the title, image, numbers, and labels), Open question (what is missing or conflicting), and Stop condition (when to stop instead of guessing). A ticket organizes observations; it never verifies a product, seller, or link.

How to use it

  1. Open the shared Google Sheet to see the listing references.
  2. Browse the twelve product category stubs from the home page.
  3. Read the three guides before relying on any clue.
  4. Write one ticket per clue and re-check the destination page.

Research ticket example — not a product record, recommendation, verification, or saved activity.
