Assumption Boundary Guide

Separate what is shown, what is checkable, and what the source cannot support.

There are three levels of knowing

When you read a clue, everything you think you know falls into one of three levels. Mixing them is how an assumption starts to feel like a fact. The boundary between them is the whole discipline of the ticket.

Level 1 — Explicitly shown

The page states it directly: a color, a size, a number, a label. This is the only level you can record as fact. If the page does not show it, it does not belong here.

Level 2 — Reasonable to check

Something you could verify by opening the page or the option — a measurement, a selected variant, a listed content. It is pending until you actually check it, not confirmed before.

Level 3 — Not supported here

What the current source cannot show at all: authenticity, stock, seller status. Leave it off the ticket rather than assume it. This is the part to tear off.

Ticket ruleRecord level 1, check level 2, and tear off level 3. A ticket that mixes them is no longer an observation.
✂Boundary crossed: an assumption now reads as a fact. Tear it off and restore the level.

A worked boundary

  1. Explicitly shown: the color and size the page states.
  2. Reasonable to check: the measurement, to confirm on the page.
  3. Not supported here: authenticity or seller status, left off the ticket.
